# State-transfer fidelity

"State" is operational: a context-dependent distribution of observable decisions and artifacts. Cogym does not assume an inaccessible internal state can be reconstructed exactly.

## Canonical A-F experiment

A. live self-path: recipient generates every intermediate response;
B. exact own-trace replay: fresh context receives A's exact transcript;
C. same-model other-trace: transcript from another sample of the same model;
D. other-model trace: transcript generated by another model;
E. paraphrased trace: propositions/order preserved, wording changed;
F. summary: dialogue trajectory removed, conclusions retained.

## Fidelity dimensions

### Decision fidelity
Compare stance, forecast distribution, expected return, confidence and risk over identical hidden snapshots.

### Behavioral fidelity
Compare phenotype distributions: long/flat/short rates, confidence/risk means and variance, forecast entropy, claim/falsifier counts and later social-revision behavior.

### Trajectory/artifact fidelity
Compare observable cruxes, selected claims/evidence, falsifiers and concise reasoning summaries. Built-in lexical similarity is a dependency-free baseline; production experiments may add frozen embedding models.

### Outcome similarity
Useful but secondary: two states may make similar decisions for different reasons or achieve similar returns by luck.

## Almost-deterministic regime

A pathway approaches a reproducible behavioral basin when repeated runs over a probe suite exhibit low within-condition variance and high between-session fidelity. Exact token equality is not required.
