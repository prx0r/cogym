# Dojo

The Dojo studies **teaching as a learned capability**.

Multiple Masters remain separate lineages. Do not average them into one teacher. Variation is information.

Useful Master metrics:

- predicted student failure accuracy;
- post-transmission capability delta;
- state-transfer fidelity;
- token/turn cost;
- transfer across model families;
- robustness across market regimes;
- novelty of successful teaching methods;
- descendant teaching quality.

A faculty roundtable may expose anonymized aggregate outcomes to Masters. Each keeps its own context and writes its own hypothesis.

When a Master's live context approaches its practical limit, a future succession experiment should compare:

- fresh Master + summary;
- exact checkpoint replay;
- predecessor-authored Master transmission;
- retrieval from the evidence graph.

Do not silently compact a Master and then claim continuity was preserved.
