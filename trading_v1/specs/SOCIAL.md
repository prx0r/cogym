# Social cognition experiments

Two-phase protocol:

1. every agent produces a private forecast;
2. peer information is revealed;
3. each agent may keep, amend or reverse;
4. private and revised decisions are scored separately.

Visibility toggles:

- decision only;
- decision + confidence/probabilities;
- full observable artifact (crux/claims/falsifiers).

Future topologies can include pairwise debate, leader/follower, anonymous peers, reputation-visible peers, rotating teams and communication budgets.

Measure both private intelligence and social integration quality. A good solo forecaster may be socially fragile; a mediocre solo agent may be excellent at integrating genuinely diagnostic peer evidence.
