# Packs

Pack kinds:

- `live_pathway`: recipient undergoes a sequential interaction;
- `checkpoint`: exact prior transcript/history artifact;
- `compiled_pathway`: shorter pathway selected through ablation against held-out probes;
- `social_policy`: pre-social cognition protocol;
- `teacher_protocol`: primes a persistent Master.

Statuses:

- `candidate`: designed but unevaluated;
- `experimental`: evaluated but not sufficiently validated;
- `certified`: tied to explicit held-out evidence IDs.

A Pack is content-addressed. Certification should be scoped to model family/version, benchmark suite, evaluator version and date. Generalization is an empirical result, not an attribute of the file.

Candidate Packs included in `/packs` are intentionally not certified.
