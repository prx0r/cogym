# High-value experiment matrix

- Context reset vs factual memory vs outcome memory vs failure-only memory.
- Neutral vs loss-salience vs missed-upside vs time-pressure induction.
- Supportive vs critical social treatment (without explicit emotion labels).
- Plain vs formal vs Bayesian vs compressed vs Socratic vs metaphor representation.
- One large model vs 2/4/8 smaller independent or communicating models.
- Anonymous peers vs identity/reputation vs decision-only vs full-rationale reveal.
- Private decision vs post-deliberation revision gain and herding loss.
- Static cognition vs evolving cognition; low vs high plasticity.
- Fitness selection vs novelty search vs mixed fitness+novelty.
- Exogenous fixed markets vs future endogenous order-book extension.
- Historical replay vs synthetic regime flips, shocks, pattern breaks and adversarial worlds.
- Pack/no-pack A/B under identical snapshots and seeds.

Primary metrics: reward, downside reward, calibration error, drawdown, regret, adaptation latency, revision gain, behavior consistency, inference cost.
